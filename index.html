IVP Project

Concept App | 2025 | Shared | Kiavi Luh

IVP is a collaborative mobile-app concept addressing the difficulty of finding parking in Isla Vista. The concept combines real-time parking information, simple interaction, user incentives, and local-business partnerships. I analyzed information relevant to our App and focused on the financial assets of our concept. I also contributed to the business and marketing potential of the parking app.

03

Expansion Project

Concept Website | 2025 | independent | Kiavi Luh

The website presents a material and design project called “Emergent Order”. It explores how structure, randomness, and collaboration can work together in the design process. The project uses LEGO bricks to create a sculpture that can change as different people contribute to it. It is inspired by Sol LeWitt’s idea of using ins tructions while still allowing room for individual interpretation. The website shows how design can evolve through participation instead of being completely controlled by one creator.
